Plain-English Legal Reference

What's legally required vs. pure dealer profit?

Only two lines on a car contract are mandated by law. Everything else is negotiable or 100% optional — and claiming a lender requires a warranty is illegal tied selling.

LEGALLY REQUIRED

State Sales Tax & DMV Title/Reg

Actual government tax (check your home ZIP + trade-in credit) and exact state DMV plate/title fees. Dealers must refund over-collected estimates.

CHECK STATE CAP

Dealer Doc / Admin Fee ($85–$1,299)

NOT a government fee. Capped in states like CA ($85), NY ($175), TX ($225). Uncapped in FL/GA/VA — negotiate the selling price down to offset it.

100% OPTIONAL • CANCELLABLE

Extended Warranty (VSC) ($2.2k–$4.8k)

Marked up 100%–200% in F&I. Full 100% refund inside free-look (30–60 days) or prorated anytime after directly through the administrator.

100% OPTIONAL • CANCELLABLE

Dealer GAP Waiver ($895–$1,495)

Identical loan/lease payoff coverage costs ~$250–$350 via your auto insurer or credit union. Cancelable for refund + early-payoff refund!

100% OPTIONAL • PURE MARKUP

Paint Shield, Nitrogen, VIN Etch, Pulse

$30–$100 wholesale items billed at $1,500–$3,500. If they say "already installed," insist on an equal discount off the selling price.

100% OPTIONAL • CANCELLABLE

Tire & Wheel / Prepaid Maintenance

Never finance future oil changes at 8% APR. Unused bundles are cancellable for full or prorated principal reduction.
